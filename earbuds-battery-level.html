Earbuds battery

See the battery level of each earbud and the case

Android often shows one battery number for your earbuds, or none. BudsPilot shows the left earbud, the right earbud and the charging case separately, on buds that report them.

How to check earbuds battery on Android

  1. Connect your earbuds to your phone over Bluetooth.
  2. Open BudsPilot. The Buds tab lists your connected pair.
  3. Read the left, right and case percentages. A lightning mark shows what is charging.

What you get

  • Left, right and case battery at a glance, on buds that report each level.
  • Earbuds battery widget for your home screen, so you do not have to open the app.
  • Battery popup when you open the case or when your buds connect.
  • Low battery alerts at 20% and 10% for each side, before one earbud cuts out mid-call.
  • Battery time left: an estimate that BudsPilot learns from how fast your own earbuds drain.
  • Listening time: how long you use your earbuds each day.

AirPods and Beats battery on Android

Using AirPods or Beats with an Android phone? BudsPilot shows their left, right and case battery too, so you get the same view you would have on an iPhone.

Per-bud battery depends on what your earbuds broadcast. Some budget buds report only a single level or none at all. The equalizer, volume controls and tools still work on every pair.

Questions

Why does Android show only one battery percentage for my earbuds?

Android reads a single battery value from most Bluetooth earbuds. Many buds also broadcast separate levels for the left bud, right bud and case, which BudsPilot reads on supported buds and shows side by side.

Is there a battery widget for Bluetooth earbuds?

Yes. BudsPilot includes an earbuds battery widget for the Android home screen.

Can I get a warning before my earbuds die?

Yes. Turn on low battery alerts and BudsPilot notifies you at 20% and 10% for each side.

How accurate is battery time left?

It is an estimate. BudsPilot learns how fast your earbuds drain while you use them, so it gets better after a few listening sessions.
