Advanced controls

Change the tap controls on earbuds that have no app

Double tap skips a track when you wanted volume? On buds with a supported chip, BudsPilot lets you decide what every tap does, and switch noise cancelling and gaming mode.

Customize earbuds touch controls

Many popular budget earbuds are built on the same Bluetooth chip family. When BudsPilot recognises it, the Advanced controls screen appears and you can remap each gesture on each side:

  • Gestures: single tap, double tap, triple tap and long press, for the left and the right bud.
  • Actions: play / pause, next track, previous track, volume up, volume down, answer call, hang up, call back, voice assistant, switch noise control, switch gaming mode, or nothing.

The new layout is saved inside the earbuds, so it keeps working on your laptop, tablet and other phones without the app.

Noise cancelling and transparency mode

If your buds have active noise cancelling, switch between Off, ANC and Transparency from the app. It only appears on buds that have noise cancelling microphones.

Gaming mode

Gaming mode lowers the sound delay for games and videos. It may use a little more battery.

Rename your buds and more

  • Give your earbuds a new Bluetooth name.
  • Change the buds' built-in EQ preset.
  • Check the firmware version.

Start Live Listen with a tap

You can also set a tap on your earbuds to start Live Listen or a recording, even with the phone screen off. Other taps keep controlling your music.

Tap remapping, noise control and gaming mode need buds with a supported chip. If yours are not supported yet, every other BudsPilot feature still works. Check supported earbuds.

Questions

Can I change touch controls on earbuds without an app?

On earbuds with a supported chip, yes. BudsPilot remaps single tap, double tap, triple tap and long press on each bud.

Can I add volume control to my earbuds taps?

Yes, on supported buds. Assign volume up and volume down to any gesture, for example triple tap on the right and left bud.

Do the new tap controls work on other devices?

Yes. The settings are stored inside the earbuds, so they work with a laptop, tablet or another phone.

How do I know if my earbuds are supported?

Connect them and open BudsPilot. If the buds show an Advanced badge, tap controls and the other advanced settings are available.
