BudsPilot privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026 · Contact: budspilot@gmail.com

The short version

BudsPilot has no account and no ads. Your buds settings stay on your phone. To improve the app and decide which earbuds to support next, it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase.

What BudsPilot reads

To show your earbuds it reads, from Android, the names, Bluetooth addresses and battery levels of paired audio devices, and the battery data some earbuds broadcast over Bluetooth. This is shown in the app and is not stored outside your phone or sent anywhere.

What BudsPilot stores

Your sound settings for each pair of buds (EQ, bass and left/right volume), your app preferences such as the style and theme, a log of when your buds connected and disconnected, and their last battery levels (for the widget and alerts). They are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect and scan) to find and read your buds; it is never used to work out your location. Modify audio settings to apply EQ and volume. A connected-device foreground service to keep your sound settings active while your buds are connected. Notifications, only if you turn on the battery popup or low-battery alerts. Run at startup, only to restart the optional background check for your buds after a reboot. On Android 11 and older, Android only allows Bluetooth scanning with the location permission, so BudsPilot asks for it there; it is used only to find your buds and your location is never read. BudsPilot does not ask for microphone, contacts, camera or storage access.

Analytics and crash reports

BudsPilot uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive: which screens and features you use; the type of earbuds you connect (chip family, model name with any personal part such as "Ali’s" removed, product IDs, firmware version and which features they support); crash reports; and your phone model, Android version, app version and a random app-instance ID. Firebase may estimate your country from your IP address. BudsPilot does not send your buds’ Bluetooth addresses, your contacts, your location or anything you listen to, and it does not use an advertising ID. This data is processed by Google under Google’s privacy policy and is used only to improve BudsPilot. You can turn it off any time in Settings › Share usage data.

Ratings

If you choose to rate BudsPilot, Google Play shows its own rating card or store page. That review is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy; BudsPilot never sees it.

Children

BudsPilot is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and only collects the anonymous usage and crash data described above, which you can turn off in Settings › Share usage data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new date. Questions about privacy? Contact the developer at budspilot@gmail.com.
