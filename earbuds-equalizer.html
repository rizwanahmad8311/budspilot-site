Earbuds equalizer

A 10-band equalizer for your Bluetooth earbuds

Budget earbuds often sound thin, boomy or harsh, and have no app to fix it. BudsPilot adds an equalizer, a bass booster and left / right volume to any pair.

Shape the sound of any earbuds

  • 10-band equalizer from 31 Hz to 16 kHz for precise control over bass, mids and treble.
  • EQ presets: Flat, Bass, Vocal, Treble, Rock, Pop, Classical and Jazz.
  • Bass booster for deeper low end on small drivers.
  • Left and right volume, separately, up to 150%. A volume booster and balance control in one, useful if one ear hears less or one bud plays quieter.
  • Applies to everything you play on those earbuds: music, video and games.
  • Saved per pair: each set of earbuds keeps its own sound and gets it back automatically on connect.

Hearing test and personal sound

A short tone test for each ear lets BudsPilot tune the equalizer to how you hear, so quiet frequencies are lifted for you and not for everyone.

The hearing test is a sound personalisation feature. It is not a medical or diagnostic test.

How to set the equalizer

  1. Connect your earbuds and open the Sound tab.
  2. Switch sound on, then pick a preset or drag the ten bands.
  3. Adjust bass boost and left / right volume to taste. Your settings are saved for that pair.

The buds' built-in EQ

On buds with a supported chip, BudsPilot can also change the equalizer stored inside the earbuds themselves, so the sound follows them to your laptop or another phone. See advanced controls.

Questions

Does the equalizer work with any Bluetooth earbuds?

Yes. The 10-band equalizer, bass booster and left / right volume work with any Bluetooth earbuds, earphones or headphones on Android 9 and newer.

Does the EQ work with Spotify, YouTube and other apps?

The equalizer is applied to what you play on those earbuds, whichever app the sound comes from.

Can I make one earbud louder than the other?

Yes. Left and right volume are separate and go up to 150%, so you can balance a quiet bud or compensate for an ear that hears less.

Will my settings be lost when I switch earbuds?

No. BudsPilot saves a sound profile for each pair and applies it when that pair connects.
