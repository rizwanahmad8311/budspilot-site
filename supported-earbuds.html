Compatibility

Earbuds that work with BudsPilot

Short answer: any Bluetooth earbuds, earphones or headphones that connect to an Android 9+ phone. How much BudsPilot can do depends on the chip inside them.

Basic · any Bluetooth earbuds

Every pair gets

  • 10-band equalizer and bass booster
  • Left and right volume up to 150%
  • Hearing test and personal sound
  • Find my earbuds sound
  • Left / right test, water eject, speaker cleaner
  • Live Listen
  • Codec, connection history and listening time
Advanced · supported chips

Supported buds also get

  • Battery for each earbud and the case
  • Tap control remapping
  • Noise cancelling and transparency mode, on buds that have them
  • Gaming mode
  • The buds' built-in EQ
  • Rename and firmware version

Budget TWS earbuds without an app

BudsPilot was built for earbuds that ship with no companion app, such as many models from Audionic, Ronin and other budget TWS brands. A large share of these buds use the same Bluetooth chip family, and on those BudsPilot unlocks the advanced controls.

Two models of the same brand can use different chips, so one may get Advanced mode and another Basic. The app tells you as soon as your buds connect.

AirPods and Beats on Android

BudsPilot shows left, right and case battery for AirPods and Beats on an Android phone, together with the equalizer and tools that work with every pair.

Bluetooth headphones and earphones

Over-ear headphones, neckbands and single earphones work in Basic mode: equalizer, bass booster, volume balance, Live Listen and the tools.

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet with Android 9 or newer.
  • Earbuds that are already paired in Android's Bluetooth settings.

Questions

Do Audionic earbuds have an app?

Many Audionic earbuds ship without a companion app. BudsPilot works with them: every pair gets the equalizer, volume controls and tools, and pairs with a supported chip also get per-bud battery and tap controls.

Do Ronin earbuds work with BudsPilot?

Yes. Ronin earbuds, like any Bluetooth earbuds, get the equalizer, bass booster, left / right volume, find my earbuds and tools. Advanced controls depend on the chip inside the model you have.

How do I know which features my earbuds get?

Connect them and open BudsPilot. Buds with an Advanced badge have per-bud battery, tap controls and the other advanced settings. All other buds run in Basic mode.

My earbuds are not supported. Can you add them?

Write to budspilot@gmail.com with the brand and model and we will look at adding support.
