Left / right earbud test, water eject and speaker cleaner

Is one earbud quieter? Are left and right swapped? Did your buds get wet? BudsPilot has a quick test and a set of cleaning tones for exactly that.

Left / right earbud test

Two quick tests, about a minute in total. First you say which side each tone plays on, then whether you hear five tones in each ear.

  • Checks that each ear plays on the correct side.
  • Checks that both buds sound the same.
  • Fixes buds that swap left and right: if the test finds the sides reversed, BudsPilot can swap them back for that pair.

Water eject for earbuds

Caught in the rain or dropped a bud in the sink? Water eject plays a low pulsing tone for 30 seconds that vibrates water out of the speaker grille.

  1. Take the buds out of your ears.
  2. Hold them with the speaker mesh facing down.
  3. Tap Start. Your volume is restored afterwards.

Speaker cleaner for dust

A dust cleaning tone helps loosen fine dust from a muffled earbud speaker. For wax or packed dirt you will still need a soft dry brush.

Bluetooth codec and connection history

  • Codec: see which Bluetooth codec your earbuds are using.
  • Connection history: when your buds connected and disconnected, useful for spotting drop-outs.
Sound can only move water and loose dust. It cannot repair earbuds that are damaged by liquid.

Questions

How do I test if my left and right earbuds are working?

Open Left / right check in BudsPilot. It plays tones on each side and asks what you hear, which takes about a minute.

My earbuds have left and right swapped. Can I fix it?

Yes. If the left / right test finds that the sides are reversed, BudsPilot can swap the channels for that pair.

How do I get water out of my earbuds?

Take the buds out of your ears, hold them with the mesh facing down and run Eject water in BudsPilot. A low pulsing tone pushes water out of the speaker.

One earbud is quieter than the other. What can I do?

Run the dust cleaning tone and clean the mesh, then use the separate left and right volume in the Sound tab to balance them.
