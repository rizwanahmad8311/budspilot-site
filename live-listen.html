Live Listen

Live Listen for Android: hear the room through your earbuds

Put your phone near the TV, the speaker at a lecture or the person across the table, and hear it more clearly in your own earbuds.

Coming soon to Google PlayCheck your earbuds

A sound amplifier for your own ears

Live Listen plays your phone's microphone on your earbuds or headphones in real time.

  • Volume boost to bring quiet voices and sounds up.
  • Noise reduction, optional, to cut steady background noise.
  • Speak: the other way round. Talk through your earbuds' microphone and your voice plays on the phone's speaker, with noise reduction and automatic anti-whistle.
  • Tap to start: set a tap on your earbuds to start Live Listen or a recording, even with the screen off.
  • Record only when you tap Record. Recordings stay on your phone; you can play, share or delete them.

How to use Live Listen

  1. Connect your earbuds and open Live Listen in BudsPilot.
  2. Tap Start and allow the microphone.
  3. Place the phone near the sound you want to hear and set the boost.

Clear about the microphone

Live Listen starts only when you tap Start, or a bud tap that you chose. Android's microphone indicator shows the whole time it is on. Nothing the microphone hears is uploaded.

Live Listen is a convenience feature for hearing your surroundings through your own earbuds. It is not a hearing aid or a medical device.

Questions

Does Android have Live Listen like the iPhone?

BudsPilot adds a Live Listen feature to Android: your phone microphone is played on your earbuds or headphones with an optional volume boost and noise reduction.

Does Live Listen work with any earbuds?

Yes. It works with any Bluetooth earbuds or headphones connected to your Android phone.

Is the sound recorded?

Only if you tap Record, or a bud tap you set to Record. Recordings are kept in the app on your phone and are never uploaded.

Is there a delay?

Bluetooth audio always adds a small delay. It is short enough for listening to speech, a TV or a lecture.
